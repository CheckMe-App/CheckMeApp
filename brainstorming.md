   - **Category:** Social Networking/Lifestyle

   - **Story:** Increase productivity and accountavblity and self awareness so the user can become the best version of themselves. Takng photos is instilling discipline in their lives with each tasks while other users can also check in on theor progress and and congulate and motivate them.
   - **Market:** Any individual who wants to work ont improving their lives.
   - **Habit:** Users should be using this on a dailbasis or until they feel like they can comeplte tasks wothout having to use an app to hold them accountable. This is a habit building app. 
   - **Scope:** V1 would allow users to create daily tasks they feel are important for their day to day lives and be able to check them off. V2 would be to take pictures and share their location so they can hold themselves accoutnable for their completed tasks and the app itself will be able to congratulate users. 2. Entertainemnet 
   - **Description**: This app will allow users the opportunity to always be abreast of the current events in their city and surrounding areas. The app will not only provide dates and locations but also live updates from the events. 
   - **Category:** Social Networking
   - **Mobile:** Mobile is essential for the live updates from these events. Users use the app to keep up to date with events happening around them. The camera is used to share images with other users.
   - **Story:** This app is perfect for the extrovert who loves going out and never knows where to go. Their life has now become simplified, just by using this app. It lets the user know the exact location, the time and the date. It also introduces them to events happening close by based on the ZIP Code that they input. Once the event has started, there are live updates so users can judge the scene of the party or event before they arrive. This is perfect for the planner or just someone unsure of where to go and what to do.
   - **Market:** Any individual who wants to know what events are being hosted nearby. 
   - **Habit:** Users can use this app daily or once a week. It is based on their want or need to find information out about an event.
   - **Scope:** This app could be technically challenging to complete by the end of this program because it uses Geo location to establish what is nearby. However, some more technical aspects may arise such as the live uploads of videos and the constant updating of the events happening.
